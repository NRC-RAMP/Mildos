### MILDOS-AREA
The MILDOS-AREA computer code calculates the radiological dose commitments received by individuals and the general population within an 80-km radius of an operating uranium recovery facility. It estimates air and ground concentrations of radionuclides for individual locations as well as for a generalized population grid, and also accounts for extra-regional population doses resulting from the transport of radon and the export of agricultural produce.
